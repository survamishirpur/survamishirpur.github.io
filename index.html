A Happy & Healthy Life

The untold story of our village

In the heart of Pratapgarh, where the morning sun first kisses the wheat fields, Surva Mishirpur wakes up to the sound of temple bells and the laughter of children. Life here is not measured by clocks but by the rhythm of the seasons—the sowing of paddy, the blooming of lotus, the fragrance of ripe mangoes.

Every household has a courtyard where grandmothers string marigolds and grandfathers narrate tales of the freedom struggle. The village is a big, extended family. Health is not just the absence of illness; it is the glow of fresh milk, the sweetness of jaggery, and the joy of shared meals under the neem tree.

Children run barefoot through the fields, climbing guava trees and swimming in the pond. The air is pure, the water sweet, and the soil fertile. It’s a place where no one sleeps hungry, and no elder is lonely. The village well remains a meeting point, and the chowpal (community space) buzzes with laughter and gossip.

Our lifestyle is simple yet rich. We grow our own vegetables, churn our own butter, and celebrate every small victory. The bond between generations is unbreakable—grandparents teach traditional games, while youngsters help with modern farming. This is the secret of our happy, healthy life.
